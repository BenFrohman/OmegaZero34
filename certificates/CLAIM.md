# Formalized claim

The claim that this repository formalizes is the lattice claim, plus the arithmetic of Engel's order formula.

## Kernel claim

The monodromy representation of Delta(3,4,infty) on Z^4 in Alpoge's note preserves a unique-up-to-scalar rational alternating form, generated by Omega0 of type (1,6). That is `OmegaZero34/Uniqueness.lean` and `OmegaZero34/TetherInterface.lean`.

## Arithmetic claim

`OmegaZero34/EngelOrder.lean` proves

    (4*m + 3*n : Rat) = 12 * ((m : Rat) / 3 + (n : Rat) / 4)

and `Int.natAbs (4*1 + 3*(-1)) = 1`.

This is the order identity in Engel, arXiv:2609.38442, Proposition 6.1. It is not a proof of the presentation, and not a proof that a constructed space is simply connected.

## Not the formalized claim

A diffeomorphism to S^6 is not a theorem of this repository. Van Kampen, the filling, and the Leray spectral sequence are not formalized here.
