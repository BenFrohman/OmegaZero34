# The unique monodromy-invariant alternating form of type (1,6)

The matrices \(T_1,T_2\) of the \((3,4,\infty)\) representation in Alpöge’s
self-hosted note `alpo.ge/s6.pdf` preserve a unique-up-to-scalar rational
alternating form, generated by

\[
\Omega_0=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0&1\\0&0&6&0\\0&-6&0&0\\-1&0&0&0\end{pmatrix}
\]

in the source basis \((\gamma,u,w,\delta)\). The form has type \((1,6)\) and
determinant \(36\). This repository checks those identities in Lean 4. It
does not treat the compactification theorem of that note.

**Correct public sentence.** The monodromy representation of
\(\Delta(3,4,\infty)\) on \(\mathbb{Z}^4\) appearing in [Alpöge,
`alpo.ge/s6.pdf`] preserves a unique-up-to-scalar rational alternating
form, generated by the explicit matrix \(\Omega_0\) of type \((1,6)\). In
particular the representation is symplectic over \(\mathbb{Q}\) but not
principally polarized over \(\mathbb{Z}\). Any functorial
monodromy-invariant symplectic structure on this local system (including a
restriction of a tether / metriplectic form) is a scalar multiple of
\(\omega_0\).

On this lattice **the tether is that form**. It is not the unipotent
logarithm \(N=T_0-I\) and it is not the Pfaffian \(\mathrm{Pf}(\Omega_0)=6\).
Those are derived from \(\Omega_0\): \(N\) is symplectic *for* the form, and
the Pfaffian is a scalar of the form.

## What is proved (sorry-free)

- \(T_1,T_2,T_0\in\mathrm{SL}(4,\mathbb{Z})\) with orders \(3\) and \(4\), and
  \(N=T_0-I\) satisfying \(N^2=0\).
- \(\Omega_0^T=-\Omega_0\), \(\det\Omega_0=36\), and
  \(T_i^T\Omega_0 T_i=\Omega_0\) for \(i=1,2,0\).
- The twelve residual pairings of a general skew matrix
  \(\Omega(a,b,c,d,e,f)\) under \(T_1\) and \(T_2\). Vanishing of those
  residuals is equivalent to \(a=b=e=f=0\) and \(d=6c\).
- Hence every rational invariant alternating form is \(\lambda\Omega_0\);
  every integral one is \(n\Omega_0\).
- \(N\in\mathfrak{sp}(\omega_0)\) and \(\ker N=\mathrm{im}\,N=
  \mathrm{span}\{\gamma,u\}\) is isotropic.
- Dual matrices \(A_j=(T_j^{-1})^T\), fixed vectors \(\varepsilon,\varepsilon'\),
  and \(\mathrm{adj}(\Omega_0)\).
- Type \((1,6)\) via the change of basis \((\gamma,\delta,u,w)\).

The uniqueness proof is equation-by-equation. There is no rank claim without
the residuals.

## What is not proved

- Existence of holomorphic period functions \(\tau,\mu,\beta\).
- Existence or holomorphicity of Alpöge’s compactification \(X\).
- \(\pi_1(X)=1\) or \(H_*(X;\mathbb{Z})\cong H_*(S^6;\mathbb{Z})\).
- That \(X\) is diffeomorphic to \(S^6\).
- Compatibility of \(\lambda\omega_0\) with the varying complex structure
  \(J(z)\).
- The geometric hypotheses H1–H4 of a Frohmanian tether (existence,
  functoriality, field-extension stability). Those remain hypotheses.
  `TetherInterface.lean` proves only the implication
  “alternating + monodromy-invariant + nondegenerate \(\Rightarrow\)
  \(\omega=\lambda\Omega_0\)”.

## How that “admits the fill”

The fillings read a lattice, a unipotent of rank two, and a vanishing plane.
With \(\omega_0\) that plane is isotropic and the logarithm is symplectic. A
functorial form is a reason some pairing exists in the family; uniqueness
names it on this example. That is the only sense in which a tether admits
the fill.

## Build

Lean 4.30.0-rc1 + Mathlib `v4.30.0-rc1`.

The sources live in this directory. They are also symlinked into
`lean-projects/NS_Millennium_Proof` so they can reuse that project's
already-built Mathlib oleans:

```
cd ~/lean-projects/NS_Millennium_Proof
lake build OmegaZero34
```

Headline theorems print their axiom traces during the build (see
`OmegaZero34/PrintAxioms.lean` and `AXIOMS.md`).

`uniqueness_over_Q` and `restriction_lemma` depend only on
`propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. Concrete \(4\times 4\)
integer identities use disclosed `native_decide`.
